# Community code of conduct

Rangabot follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing and publication of another person's private data are unacceptable.
Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate
these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
maintainer contact rather than opening a public issue containing sensitive data.
