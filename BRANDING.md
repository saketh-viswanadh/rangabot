## Primary identity assets

The Founder-approved public repository mark is tracked as
`public/brand/rangabot-primary-512.png`, with the broader responsive usage
hierarchy documented in its
[`BRAND-ASSET-MANIFEST.md`](docs/media/brand/BRAND-ASSET-MANIFEST.md). Larger
icon sources, smaller touch/preview variants, chat marks and the conversation
spark remain in their owning product or company asset paths rather than being
duplicated in repository documentation. Adding the 512 px public mark here does
not claim that an application icon, website favicon, social preview or product
runtime has been deployed.

The primary mark was created for Rangabot with OpenAI image generation from
Founder-directed golden-retriever/R identity prompts. No private photograph or
third-party logo was used as an input. The Founder reviewed and approved the
direction on 2026-08-14. The compact chat glyph was redrawn from scratch as a
project SVG after the Founder supplied a generic negative-space
golden-retriever image as structural inspiration; no source pixels or exact
geometry were copied. Repository integration copies the finalized 512 px
primary mark byte-for-byte without regenerating or editing it.

The separate CC BY 4.0 statement for `public/ranga/` has not been extended to
these new identity files. They are proprietary Rangabot identity assets
approved for official product use. Rangabot reserves all trademark rights in
the name and identity set, and no redistribution license is granted for these
files. Do not infer a grant from the Apache-2.0 code and documentation terms or
the CC BY 4.0 terms for the earlier Ranga artwork.

## Rangabot name

The Apache and Creative Commons licenses do not grant trademark rights in the
Rangabot name. Forks and derivative projects may truthfully say “based on
Rangabot” or “compatible with Rangabot,” but should use a distinct product name
and must not imply endorsement or official status.

Unmodified community builds of this repository may identify themselves as
Rangabot. Materially modified distributions should use a distinct primary name
unless the project owner grants written permission.
